ACME WIDGETS PVT LTD - IT SECURITY POLICY (FAKE SAMPLE DATA)
Access level: PUBLIC (all employees)

Passwords: Use at least 12 characters. Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff.
Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Use the company password manager.

Multi-factor authentication: MFA is required for email, VPN and the cloud console.

Laptops: All company laptops must have disk encryption turned on and the screen lock set to 5 minutes.
Lost devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 2 hours.

Phishing: Report suspicious emails with the "Report Phish" button. Do not click unknown links.
The IT helpdesk will never ask for your password by email or chat.

Software: Only install software from the approved software catalogue.
